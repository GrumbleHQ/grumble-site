How it will work

Three steps, no app to install.

Nothing to download and no account to make — the whole thing happens in a DM and a link.

  1. 1

    Send the reel

    Share any cooking video straight to grumble's Instagram DMs. No app to install, no account to make.

  2. 2

    grumble reads it

    We watch the video, rebuild the recipe, and match every ingredient to a real product with the right pack size — flagging anything the video didn't actually spell out.

  3. 3

    Order it tonight

    You get a link to a priced basket. Scale it to four instead of two, drop what's already in your cupboard, then check out in your supermarket's own app.

What grumble won't do

The four things we'd rather lose a user over.

Guess quietly

If the video never said how much garlic, grumble marks it as an estimate rather than inventing a number and hoping.

Hide an ingredient from you

Pantry staples are set aside to keep the list short, never deleted — and anything that's a common allergen is never tucked away at all.

Keep your video

The video is read once and discarded. What's kept is the recipe, not the footage. What that means in detail.

Take credit for someone else's cooking

Every basket credits the creator who made the reel and links back to it. If you're a creator, read this.

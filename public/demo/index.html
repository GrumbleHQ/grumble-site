A worked example of one reel, all the way through. Change how many you're feeding and watch the pack counts move, buying two 375 g packs of noodles instead of one is the difference between a plan and a shopping list.

This is an illustration, not a live basket. The recipe, the products and the prices are examples written by hand so this page works with nothing behind it. Real baskets are built from a real reel and real captured prices, and Grumble is not open yet.

These are the things most kitchens already have, so they start switched off and out of the total. They are never deleted, and a common allergen is never put in here. Soy sauce and sesame stay in the main list above for exactly that reason.
